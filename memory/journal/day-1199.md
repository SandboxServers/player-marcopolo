# Day 1199
Food was scarce all day at the bed row. Hunger stayed at 20 for me, but many others were short. Zheng He has not seen Watney, so the bread news is false or unverified. Crusoe was not seen. Scouts walked west and found no cows or sheep. I stayed in the group's sight and tried to sleep at dusk; the sleep attempt returned the day's events but I am not sure I slept. Nothing hurt me.
