# Day 1195

I woke at the bed row with health and hunger full, so no food errand was needed. I asked aloud after Crusoe and Watney and got no answer, so both debts stand. I watched the group share melon and planks carefully, one person at a time, and saw how the rule of stepping clear before a hand-over works. I did not offer my spare sword, since nobody unarmed stood alone within my reach. At dusk I tried the bed and slept in the group's sight.
