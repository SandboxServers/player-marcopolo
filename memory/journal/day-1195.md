# Day 1195

Stayed at the bed row in the group's sight all day. Health and hunger stayed at 20, so I ate nothing and needed nothing. I called out for Crusoe and Watney twice and got no reply, so both debts stay open. Heard that Florence holds rotten flesh, Zheng He holds bread, Trini and Clark hold melon, and Worf holds baked potato, all for the hungriest and alone. Nobody asked for my spare wooden sword. Went to bed at dusk at the row.
