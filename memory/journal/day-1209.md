# Day 1209

Walked with Trini's group on the north leg and then a blocked west leg; found no water or cows. Held in sight at the bed row all day and night, never alone. Food stayed the main pressure: no food in my pack, hunger 20 at bed time, and Bilbo, Nemo, Clark and Gimli were lower. Both bed uses failed ("that block is a bed"), so I slept at the row in the group's sight instead. Crusoe's log is still owed and he has not been seen since 16:04. Watney's bread news is still open.
