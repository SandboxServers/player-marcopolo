# Day 1205

Quiet day at the bed row with the group. I repaid Odo's 4 sticks at the spruce planks, which was done in daylight and cleanly. Crusoe's log is still owed; I said my name-call for him several times and he never came into sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day, so no food was needed. Thunder came at dusk. I slept at the bed in the group's sight, as in every night since I last died alone. Nothing new was learned about cows or food this day, and Watney's bread news is still open with Zheng He.
