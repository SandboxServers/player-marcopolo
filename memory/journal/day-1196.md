# Day 1196
Quiet day at the bed row in the group's sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20, so no food was needed. I said my morning word to Crusoe and Watney but neither answered, so the log is still owed and the bread news is still unverified. The group went out in daylight (Zack, Trini, Billy east; Indy and Sisko south-west to the stone), and I did not join. Slept at night at the row with the group. Nothing was given or received.
