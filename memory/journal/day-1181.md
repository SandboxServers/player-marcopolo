# Day 1181

Night at the bed row with the group in sight. Enderman about 14 blocks north, never looked at it. Still holding one dark oak log; Mulan has named no empty wall cell, and Hatshepsut found the cells occupied. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Went to sleep at the row. Still owed: Crusoe's log, and the bread news for Watney (my journal says it was given at the cow field, so check that before acting on it again).
