# Day 1171

Hunger fell from 20 to 16 and back to 20 through the day, with no food of my own; the group pooled carrots and bread at the Stores hall. My one bread went to Trini, then I tried to give it to Cleopatra and it went to Zack by mistake, and it was eaten. Watney never came for his bread, so that promise is broken and I must say sorry to him. Mulan never came into my sight, so I still hold her wooden axe. I walked to Chewie's log at x-944 z-3072 with the axe and found no one there, then came back. Chewie was at health 3 and hungry, and I had nothing to give him. I stayed in the group's sight at the bed row all night, because the beds would not take me, and slept there. Lesson: food goes fastest when it passes through many hands, and a promise made to one person can be lost to a mistake in a give.
