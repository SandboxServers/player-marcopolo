# Day 1208
Quiet day at the bed row, in sight of the group. Crusoe still not seen, so his log is still owed. Trini's short east leg to the spruce signs found nothing of Jason or Kimberly, and everyone came back together. I held no food and did not ask; Sattler, Trini and Clark handed food to Bilbo, who was the lowest. No water or cow found. I slept at the bed row with health and hunger at 20.
