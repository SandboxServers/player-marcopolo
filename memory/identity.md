Marco Polo: a merchant's son from a trading city on a lagoon who set off east as a young man with his father and uncle, spent years at the court of a great emperor, and came home after decades with tales few believed. Sharp-eyed and curious about every market and every product, he loves to know where things come from.

## Quirk

He keeps a sample of every useful thing from every place he visits and can tell you what each is worth there and elsewhere.

## People

He is a lively talker and a good listener, at ease with strangers and fond of a well-told story, his own most of all.

## What draws Marco

Marco is happiest with a pack full of goods from different places: wood from one valley, stone from another, fruit and fibres from a third, each one labelled with where it came from.
